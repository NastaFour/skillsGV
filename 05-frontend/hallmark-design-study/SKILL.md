# 🏛️ Hallmark v2.0, Hue Hero Stage, Stitch `extract-design-md` & UIZZE `image-to-ui`

Unified synthesis of **Nutlope / Together AI `hallmark` v2.0** (21 macrostructures, SaaS Section Deck, finish gates & `P-H-E-S-R-V` critique), **Dominik Martin `hue`** (2-layer Hero Stage physics & perceptual brand capture), **Google Labs `stitch-skills` `extract-design-md`** (4-phase static code-to-`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`), and **UIZZE `image-to-ui`** (1:1 render-compare loop). Delegates the canonical 3-line `Design Read` and Archetypes `A–H` to [`../design-driven/SKILL.md`](../design-driven/SKILL.md).

## 🚫 Hard Invariants (The Ethical & Anti-Slop Floor)

1. **ETHICAL STRUCTURAL EXTRACTION ONLY**: Extract abstract design DNA (grid cadence, type ratio, OKLCH polarity, transition physics) — NEVER copy trademarked logos, copyrighted copy, or proprietary illustrations. Scan untrusted external assets/HTML with [`prompt-shield`](../../03-ai-ml/prompt-shield/SKILL.md) before analysis.
2. **CONCRETE TOKENS & UNIFIED `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`**: Emit the 3-line `Design Read` (`design-driven`), sync discoveries into `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, and use semantic CSS variables (`--surface-*`, `--text-*`, `--accent-*`, `--border-*` in OKLCH/HSL).
3. **CONTEXT-HARMONIZED ANTI-SLOP GATES (Never Silly Cages)**:
   - **Gate 46a & 54 (No Wireframe Box-Walls or Eyebrow/Pill Confetti)**: Ban inert marketing eyebrows above headlines (`FEATURES`, `• Live monitoring`), decorative button arrows (`Learn more →`), decorative status dots, tutorial guide cards explaining obvious UI, >2 badges per card, and **wireframe box-walls** (wrapping every label, inline keyword, counter, or link in `0–2px` `1px`-bordered rectangles).
   - **Gate 63 (Template Punctuation — Middots AND Slashes)**: Ban `Meta A · Meta B` chains AND `"A / B"` slash-concatenated UI labels (`"Application Layer / Hypertext Transfer"`, `"0 notes / 0 highlights"`, `"j / k"` floating chrome). Use natural copy or clean spatial grouping.
   - **Gate 67 (Tracked-Caps — Context-Scoped)**: Ban decorative tracked-caps labels (`uppercase` + `letter-spacing >= 0.08em` at `<= 15px`) on hero eyebrows, marketing badges, and feature cards; **explicitly allow** for dense B2B table `<th>` headers (`ui-ux-pro-max`), Archetype B (`Spec-Sheet`) / hardware instrument coordinates, or `[Beyond-Baseline: <reason>]`.
   - **Gate 64 (Monospace Zoning — Harmonized with `taste-skill`)**: Ban `font-mono` on narrative body prose, marketing copy, brand wordmarks, and decorative `[P01]` gutter codes; **explicitly allow/mandate** `font-mono` + `tabular-nums` for `<code>`/`<kbd>`, quantitative telemetry/financial metrics/CVE-IDs/timestamps, and Archetypes B & E.
   - **Gate 58 & 70**: Hold heroes to a `<= 80`-word above-the-fold budget (`1280x800`) and NEVER render fake `"Subscribed"` / `"Sent"` feedback on unwired forms.

> **Greenfield Phase 3 Audit vs Reference Cloning**: On greenfield builds (`design-driven` Phase 3), use **Hallmark v2 Gates (`19–85`), Finish Checks (`F1–F26`), and `P-H-E-S-R-V`** directly against the live rendered app (skip `study` URL cloning and UIZZE screenshot cloning). Use `study`, Stitch `extract-design-md`, and UIZZE `image-to-ui` only when reverse-engineering a vetted reference URL, codebase, or screenshot.

## 🧭 Four Complementary Workflows (`Hallmark v2` × `Hue` × `Stitch` × `UIZZE`)

### 1. Hallmark v2.0 (`study` | `build` | `audit` | `redesign`)
- **21 Macrostructures & SaaS Section Deck ("Deviate Twice")**: Pick one of the 21 architectural macrostructures via its 6-axis fingerprint (`Heading placement | Body composition | Divider language | Button voice | Image treatment | Reveal pattern`). On product sites, start with a standard section deck and **deviate twice** (drop 1 predictable strip like the generic bottom CTA band; promote 1 concrete proof/spec/pricing artifact higher).
- **Pre-Emit Critique (`P-H-E-S-R-V` 1–5)**: Score **Philosophy, Hierarchy, Execution, Specificity, Restraint, Variety** alongside the 26 Finish Checks (`F1–F26`) before final delivery.

### 2. Hue (`dominikmartn/hue`) 2-Layer Hero Stage Physics & Icon Tracks
- Decompose heroes into `background field` × `hero subject` × `relation layer` using the **Physical Compatibility Matrix** (never give a `luminous` glow object a floor contact shadow; never emit an `emissive` halo around an opaque physical object; never fake complex hardware 3D with CSS divs). Capture iconography in **Dual-Track** form (*Observed brand geometry* vs *Open-source fallback kit* e.g. Lucide/Phosphor).

### 3. Google Stitch `extract-design-md` (Static Code-to-`DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`, Zero Build)
1. **Discovery**: Inspect `style-dictionary`/tokens → `tailwind.config.*`/`@theme` → `:root` CSS vars → component utilities (`react`, `vue`, `svelte`, `angular`, plain CSS).
2. **Consolidate & Name**: Deduplicate near-identical hexes (`#2C2C2C` vs `#333`), map OKLCH roles with evocative functional names (`"Deep Muted Teal-Navy"`), and capture type hierarchy + spacing rhythm into `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`.

### 4. UIZZE `image-to-ui` (1:1 Render-Compare-Correct Loop)
- From a vetted reference screenshot: extract layout skeleton + exact proportions → build with semantic tokens → capture/inspect rendered output across breakpoints → run iterative visual correction until geometry, alignment, and hierarchy converge.
